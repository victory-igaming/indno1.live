3. Make overlays stick to the actual video content, not black bars

This is extremely important.

Right now, when the video aspect ratio creates empty black bars or unused frame space, some overlays drift into those black areas because they are positioned relative to the outer player container instead of the actual visible video content.

Required fix

All overlays must be positioned and scaled relative to the actual rendered video rectangle, not just the full container.

That means:

if the YouTube content is letterboxed
or pillarboxed
or centered with empty side spaces

then overlays must stay aligned to the visible video portion only.

Expected behavior
Logos, lower thirds, banners, and ads should remain attached to the actual video
They should never float into black margins
In fullscreen and normal mode, overlays should stay visually locked to the stream content
Technical expectation

Compute the rendered video box inside the container using the stream aspect ratio and container size, then anchor overlays to that calculated region.

4. Make overlays responsive and scalable

Overlay size and position must scale responsively across:

mobile portrait
mobile landscape
tablets
desktop
fullscreen mode
Required behavior
Overlays should preserve proportional positioning
Overlays should grow and shrink with the visible video area
Existing dimensions should be converted or interpreted in a responsive way
Ads/logos should not overflow or become tiny/unusable
For media overlays

If an overlay contains an image or video ad:

intelligently scale it based on the dominant dimension
ensure it fits its intended placement cleanly
avoid stretching distortion
avoid overflow outside the visible video content
5. Auto-hide player action buttons when idle

The Mute/Unmute and Fullscreen buttons should have a cleaner experience.

Desktop behavior
Show buttons on hover over the player
Hide them when the user is idle or not hovering
Mobile behavior
Show buttons when the user taps/interacts with the player
Hide them again after a few seconds of inactivity
General rules
Smooth fade/slide animation
Buttons should never obstruct essential overlays
Buttons should reappear immediately on interaction
Buttons should still remain accessible and usable
